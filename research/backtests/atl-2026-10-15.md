## Reading

- **All-time lows lag.** The typical all-time-low buy was down 7.7% after 60 sessions and 17.3% after 250 (medians),
  63% fell another 20%+ within 60 sessions (26% on a random session of the same stocks) and 29% did a reverse split
  within two years (3%).
- **No company news is better than news, but not an edge.** No-news lows: median -4.0% after 60 sessions, -3.2%
  after 250, -3.6 ± 5.4% against SPY over 60; 59% fell another 20%+ first. Offerings are the worst news (median
  -61% after 250 sessions).
- **The positive no-news means come from crash days.** Lows on days SPY fell 1%+ (mostly March 2020): +15.2 ± 14.7%
  after 60 sessions, +5.0 ± 8.8% against SPY. Stock-specific lows on calm days: -8.5 ± 4.3% against SPY over 60
  sessions, median -10.5%. Outside Feb 15-Apr 30 2020, no-news lows lagged SPY by 6.2 ± 4.1% over 60 sessions and
  13.7 ± 7.8% over 250.
- **Price matters.** No-news lows at $5+ did what a random session of the same stocks did (-1.0 ± 4.3% against SPY
  over 60 sessions vs -1.3 ± 0.7%), with twice the chance of another 20% fall. Under $1: median -23% after 60
  sessions and -35% after 250, 81% fell another 20%+, 60% reverse-split within two years.
- **Survivors only.** Stocks that died after their lows are missing, so the true all-time-low returns are worse,
  most of all under $1.
