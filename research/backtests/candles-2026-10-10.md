## Reading

- Patterns are common: 0.9 to 12.9 a day across the 63 names, so one shows up somewhere every day. Frequency is not the problem.
- None tells the next hour: gross means sit within 0.11% of the baseline (+0.00%, 48% up) while these names move 1-2% an hour. Bullish patterns ran slightly below the baseline (hammer -0.02, bullish engulfing -0.04, three white soldiers -0.07) and bearish ones slightly above (three black crows +0.10 in both halves, big red bar +0.07 in both, dark cloud cover +0.07): a small bounce after weakness, the opposite of the textbook reading, and no bigger than the round trip's cost (0.10% at $20+, 0.40% at $5-20).
- The +0.5% scalp: on 5-minute bars the baseline reaches +0.5% first 40% of the time and -0.5% 56% (a limit sale needs a trade a cent through it, a stop triggers on touch): -0.08% a trade before costs, -0.33% after. No bullish pattern does better (30-42% target hits). Without a stop the target is hit 58-68% of the time, but the misses run to the close and the average is -0.25% to -0.58% after costs: a high hit rate with a losing average.
- Scale: the $2,000 target needs +110% in the 18 sessions left, 4.2% of the whole account a session. +0.5% on a 25% position adds 0.125% to the account; even all-in, 0.5% a day compounds to +9.4% by Nov 4.
- Verdict: no rule change. The one effect that shows, a bounce after weakness, is what the night sleeve (RUNBOOK 2e) trades on a daily scale, where the move is large enough to beat the costs.
