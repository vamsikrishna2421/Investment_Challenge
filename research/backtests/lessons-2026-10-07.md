# Lessons of Oct 7, tested before adopting

On Wed Oct 7 two paper positions were stopped at a weak open (POET's stop sat 0.37 ATR under its entry; UMAC, the
one buy taken without a dip, lost most), and the morning scans showed two-day moves as "pre-market" moves. The day
suggested seven rule changes. Each was replayed against the live rules the same day (`scripts/replay.py --set
lessons` for the paper rules, `--set realbook` for an approximation of the real book's rules: DAY limits at support
tested 3+ times, R:R 2.5+, 3 positions, no run buys). The adoption rule (RUNBOOK 2d): beat the live rules on the
mean and the worst window of the separate hourly 22-session windows without losing on the 5-minute windows.

Samples: the last 59 sessions on 5-minute bars (one path, and 30 overlapping 30-session windows) and 700 sessions on
hourly bars (one path, and 33 separate 22-session windows). Reports: `research/replay/*-2026-10-06-*-lessons.md` and
`*-realbook.md`.

## Decisions

- Adopted: size on the stop distance plus a 1% gap allowance (`levels.GAP_PCT`, used by `levels.py check`,
  `orders.py plan` and `real.py`). In the 700-session replay half the stops gapped through at the open and filled
  on average 1.06% under their price (median 0.2%, 90th percentile 2.9%, worst 15.3%).
- Adopted (mechanics, no test needed): Robinhood cancels a sell stop priced above the market, so `real.py` sells a
  position whose price is already at or under its stop (`stop_exit`); pre-market moves are measured from the last
  close (`portfolio.ref_close`), not from Yahoo's previousClose, which is two sessions back before the open.
- Rejected: entries only from 9:45 (support limits filled on gap-down opens often catch the low of the day), a
  minimum distance between entry and stop, skipping names down 1.0-1.5+ ATR on the day, selling gapped stops at
  9:55. Watch: entries only 0.25+ ATR under the prior close led over the last 60 sessions and lost over 2.8 years.
- The S&P gate stays in the real book and out of the paper book.

## What the exercise taught

Seven candidate rules came out of one morning; one survived the test, two mechanical fixes needed none, and the change
that looked most obvious (wait until 9:45 before buying) was the worst in the real-book test. A vivid trade is a
hypothesis, not a rule.
