## Deep dive (written Tue Sep 29, 11:45 PM ET; asked by the user, not a candidate)

**Why it keeps falling: the company is the biggest seller of its own stock.**
- Class A shares went from 323.4M (Dec 31) to 581.5M (Jun 30), then 70.8M more were sold through the at-the-market program
  between Jul 1 and Aug 13 for $9.2M (about $0.13 a share before the split). The agents are Spartan Capital and Wilson-Davis,
  under a $50M program ([10-Q, Aug 19](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/896493/000121465926010549/hd81126810q.htm)).
  2.5 billion shares are authorized.
- The 1-for-5 reverse split on Aug 25 reset the price; the selling continued (136M shares now, about 680M in pre-split terms).
- Six reverse splits since 2019 (1-for-20, 1-for-40, 1-for-300, 1-for-25, 1-for-35, 1-for-5) multiply to about 1-for-1.05
  billion. Split-adjusted, the stock was $52.50 in April 2024 and is $0.16 now (-99.7%).
- At $0.16 it is below $0.20 again, the level NYSE American treats as a low selling price; the usual cure is another reverse split.

**Who is ahead of common shareholders (June 30).** Total liabilities $243.5M, plus $31.6M of new Bitcoin-backed DeFi (Morpho)
borrowings after the quarter, of which $99.3M in notes is due within a year. Preferred stock carries a $90.9M liquidation
preference and gets monthly cash dividends (13% Series D, 10% Series E). Going-concern doubt: "substantial doubt ... has
not been alleviated." Net loss $49.1M in the first half.

**The fine print in the company's pitch.** The Sep 29 release says Bitcoin plus cash of about $56M equals 153% of the
common's market value ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/hyperscale-data-held-approximately-56-million-of-bitcoin-cash-and-restricted-cash-as-of-september-27-2026-representing-approximately-153-of-the-market-capitalization-of-its-common-stock-302892268.html)).
It counts restricted cash and Bitcoin that is pledged as collateral for the DeFi loans, and it ignores about $275M of
liabilities and $91M of preferred claims that come first. Comparing gross assets with the value of the last claim in line is
meaningless.

**Governance.** Milton "Todd" Ault's Ault & Company controls it (Class B shares; 13D amendment no. 20 filed Sep 28). In June,
while under going-concern doubt, the company lent Ault & Company $5.0M at 8.5%. Insiders bought about $1.0M of shares in
six months while the company sold about $9M in six weeks.

**What the price assumes.** At about $22M the market values common at roughly its book value after preferreds (about $19M).
That book value rests on soft assets (a private-equity book, cranes, hotels, the unbuilt Michigan AI campus) and falls with
every quarter of losses. Revenue guidance of $180-200M is not the problem; per-share value is.

**What would change the view:** the at-the-market program ended; the Michigan campus funded with project debt or a partner
instead of common stock; the going-concern language gone; a flat share count for two quarters.

**Verdict.** Avoid in both books. It fails every filter the scanner uses: volatility 159%, 22 days of +/-15%, share count up
418% in a year, a reverse split within 18 months, going-concern doubt, under $1, under $100M. It isn't oversold; it's
oversupplied. Shorting isn't available (cash accounts, and 31% of the float is already short, so squeezes are violent).
