## Deep dive (written)
Wed Sep 30, 2026, about 5 PM ET, after the close. Not held in either book.

Notes on the numbers above:
- The peers were passed by hand, because the automatic screen returned clinical-stage biotechs. CPRX was also requested but returned no data.
- The table's EV ($1.40B) uses the pre-jump price. At $3.90 it is about $1.58B.

**What changed.**
- Wednesday's trial opinion in *United Therapeutics v. Liquidia* (D. Del., C.A. 23-975-RGA; [opinion](https://www.ded.uscourts.gov/sites/ded/files/opinions/23-975_6.pdf)) found that Liquidia's Yutrepia infringes claims 1 and 14 of UTHR's '327 patent. The patent covers treating PH-ILD with inhaled treprostinil, including by dry powder inhaler, and expires Feb 3, 2042.
- It is a ruling on the merits, not just the denial of Liquidia's motion to strike. No remedy has been set yet:
  - UTHR has asked for relief that would restrict Yutrepia's availability.
  - Liquidia will remove PH-ILD from its label and appeal.
  - The form of judgment is due in about a week. Details are in UTHR-2026-09-30.md.
- MNKD rose 16.8% to $3.90 on 3.6x normal volume because it sits on the other side of Tyvaso DPI, Yutrepia's direct competitor. Verified in the filings:
  - MNKD manufactures Tyvaso DPI for UT under a supply agreement that runs to Dec 31, 2031 and renews automatically.
  - Under the 2018 license, it earns a 10% royalty on Tyvaso DPI net sales. It sold 1 point of that royalty to Sagard in Dec 2023 for $150M upfront and keeps 9%. Sagard receives 1% of net sales from Oct 1, 2023 to Dec 31, 2042 ([10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/899460/000119312526335054/mnkd-20260630.htm); [Sagard terms](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/mannkind-corp.:-sagard-healthcare-purchases-1-royalty-in-tyvaso-dpi-net-sales)).
- Q2 2026 revenue was $109.4M (+43%) ([Q2 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/899460/000119312526335016/mnkd-ex99_1.htm); [10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/899460/000119312526335054/mnkd-20260630.htm)):
  - From UT: royalties $32.4M (+4%), manufacturing under the supply agreement $29.6M, and ralinepag DPI development work $4.9M. UT was $66.9M, or 61% of revenue, against 70% a year earlier.
  - Royalty growth slowed (+9% in Q1, +4% in Q2) after Yutrepia launched in June 2025, and UTHR blames competing therapies. The Q2 royalty is 9.9% of UTHR's $326.6M Tyvaso DPI sales.
  - Other products: Afrezza $17.0M (-7%), Furoscix $22.2M, V-Go $2.8M.
  - The royalty and manufacturing supply together are 57% of revenue and, by my estimate, about 60% of gross profit.

**How big relative to MNKD.**
- Sensitivity: each $100M of annual Tyvaso DPI sales gives MNKD $10M of royalty revenue ($9M kept after Sagard). It also brings about $8M of manufacturing revenue, at roughly half gross margin. (In Q2, product sales to UT were $27.0M on $326.6M of DPI sales.)
- Yutrepia's PH-ILD half is about $340M a year at the Q2 run rate: $170.4M a quarter, half of it for PH-ILD according to Liquidia.
  - If Tyvaso DPI won back half of it, MNKD would gain about $22M a year of gross profit ($15M net royalty plus $7M manufacturing).
  - If Tyvaso DPI won back all of it, about $44M.
  - MNKD's trailing EBITDA is $19.5M, so a clean recapture could double or triple it.
- At 8-10x, that is $0.55-0.69 a share for half and $1.10-1.38 for all. This is my arithmetic. It assumes the patients move to DPI rather than to nebulized Tyvaso, on which MNKD earns nothing.
  - Wednesday's +$0.56 (+$180M of market value) priced roughly the half case.
- Another yardstick is Sagard's price. Sagard paid $150M for one royalty point in Dec 2023.
  - At that price, the nine points MNKD keeps would be worth about $1.35B, about 86% of MNKD's roughly $1.58B EV.
  - So MNKD trades largely as a leveraged Tyvaso DPI royalty. Protecting DPI's PH-ILD business protects most of the equity value.
- Sagard's price assumed faster growth:
  - Its $50M milestone needs trailing DPI sales of $1.9B by Dec 31, 2026. Sales were about $650M in the first half of 2026, so it will almost certainly not be paid.
  - The fallback $45M milestone needs $2.3B by Sep 30, 2027.

**Firm vs contingent.**
- The trial-level finding is firm. Everything that turns it into MNKD revenue is not:
  - how broad the remedy is (form of judgment about Oct 7);
  - whether any injunction is put on hold while Liquidia appeals;
  - the Federal Circuit appeal (about a year last time);
  - whether doctors keep using a PAH-only Yutrepia for PH-ILD off-label;
  - whether switching patients choose DPI or nebulized Tyvaso.
- UT must give MNKD its purchase-quantity notice under the supply agreement by Oct 15 each year (10-Q), but the notice is not published (not found).
- Yutrepia's PAH business, the other half and about $340M a year, is untouched.
- A tail risk to the royalty: Liquidia's '494 patent suit against Tyvaso DPI (the patent expires May 5, 2037). It is on hold until the North Carolina trade-secret trial, set for January 2027 ([UTHR 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1082554/000108255426000027/uthr-20260630.htm)).

**One-offs.**
- Q2 revenue included two items that won't repeat at that size:
  - $4.9M of ralinepag DPI development revenue. UT paid $5M in Aug 2025 and $5M in Apr 2026, and $3.1M is left to recognize.
  - $2.6M of previously deferred supply revenue.
- The income statement books the full 10% royalty. Sagard's point is paid out against the royalty liability ($6.5M in the first half).
- The Q2 net loss was $19.0M (-$0.06 a share), after $11.9M of interest and a $5.0M charge for the rising value of the acquisition's contingent payments.
- Q3 cash takes the payout of about $44.8M on the contingent value rights owed to former scPharmaceuticals holders. It was triggered by the Furoscix ReadyFlow approval on Jul 23, 2026.

**Dilution and financing: the main risk.**

  - The convertible notes were retired Mar 4, 2026 ($35.5M in cash plus 569,023 shares).
  - Also on the balance sheet: a $147.8M Sagard royalty liability, a $103.0M financing liability, and up to $14.9M of remaining contingent payments.

**Competition.**
- Tyvaso DPI and Yutrepia keep competing in PAH. Yutrepia had about 30% of inhaled treprostinil net revenue in Q2.
- UTHR's oral ralinepag (FDA decision due Jun 24, 2027) may take PAH patients from inhaled therapy. MNKD would earn a 10% royalty and up to $35M of milestones on a ralinepag DPI (MNKD-1501, IND planned by year-end).
- Insmed's TPIP is in development.
- Afrezza (inhaled insulin) is shrinking (-7%) despite the May 29, 2026 pediatric approval.
- Furoscix grew 43% quarter on quarter to $22.2M. The ReadyFlow autoinjector was due to launch in late August.
