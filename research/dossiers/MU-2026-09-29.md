## Deep dive (written Tue Sep 29, 11:30 PM ET; reports Wed Sep 30 after the close)

**What changed.** A memory shortage turned Micron into the most profitable company in its peer set. Fiscal Q3 (to May 28):
revenue $41.46B (from $23.86B the quarter before and $9.30B a year earlier), non-GAAP gross margin 84.9%, EPS $25.11. It
signed multi-year "Strategic Customer Agreements" to make that durable. Guidance for fiscal Q4 (the quarter reported tomorrow):
revenue $50.0B plus or minus $1.0B, gross margin about 86%, EPS $31.00 plus or minus $1.00 ([release, 8-K ex. 99.1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/723125/000072312526000013/a2026q3ex991-pressrelease.htm)).
HBM3E and HBM4 are reported fully booked through 2027; UBS models blended HBM prices up about 76% in fiscal 2027.

**The fine print.**
- Fiscal Q4 has 14 weeks, Q1 has 13. A Q1 guide that looks flat quarter on quarter is about 7% growth per week. The
  headline comparison will look weaker than the business.
- The Street is above the guide: consensus EPS $31.82 (range $30.20-37.44), revenue $51.47B, above the top of the range.
  A result inside the guided range reads as a miss.
- Insiders sold $261M in six months (18 sales, CEO included, at $906-990); no insider bought.
- A union at the Taoyuan plant wants 15% of operating profit for bonuses; a strike vote is planned for early October.

**Competition and the cycle.** SK Hynix and Samsung are adding HBM4 capacity. Memory has always been a cycle: record margins
bring record capex, then oversupply. At 6.6x forward EPS ($161.49) against 15-52x for the rest of the semiconductor list, the
market is pricing these as peak-cycle earnings, not as a new normal. The peer-multiple arithmetic in the table ($2,500-8,400)
is meaningless here: applying Nvidia's or Broadcom's multiples to cyclical peak earnings is the wrong model.

**What the price assumes.** $1,065 is 15% below the $1,255 high. That is about 34x annualized fiscal Q3 EPS, but about 8.5x
annualized Q4 guidance. The stock needs a Q1 guide at or above the consensus path (DRAM revenue about $42.8B, +12% quarter on quarter,
per Visible Alpha) and signs that the customer agreements lock in prices into 2027.

**Tomorrow's trade in numbers.** The options price a 7.6% move either way (Oct 2 straddle $80.72 on $1,065; implied vol 91%).
MUU (2x) prices 14.7%. Over the last 15 reports the average next-day move was 8.1% (median 7.2%), so the options are fairly
priced: no volatility edge in either direction. Micron beat EPS estimates in each of the last 12 quarters, yet the stock fell
the next day 7 times. The beat is expected; the guide and the tone decide the move.

**What decides it:** the fiscal Q1 revenue and margin guide on a per-week basis, pricing and duration in the customer agreements,
2027 capex (more supply is the bear case), the HBM4 share at the lead customer, and the Taoyuan labor vote.
