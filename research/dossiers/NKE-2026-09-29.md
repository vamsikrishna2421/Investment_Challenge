and EMEA shrinking, North America growing. Management: "sell-through remains challenged"
([release, 8-K ex. 99.1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320187/000032018726000076/q4fy26exhibit991er.htm)).
Consensus for fiscal Q1 on Thursday: EPS $0.44 on revenue of $11.33B (-3.2%), Greater China about -12.6%.

**The fine print.**
- Q4 gross margin of 49.2% included about 900 basis points ($986M) from the expected refund of IEEPA tariffs after the
  Supreme Court ruling. EPS of $0.72 included $0.52 of it: underlying Q4 EPS was about $0.20.
- Trailing EPS ($2.07) and possibly the forward consensus ($2.16) carry refund effects. On clean earnings the stock is more
  expensive than 16.6x suggests.
- Insiders bought: 5 open-market purchases ($3.7M) in six months, against 9 small sales ($1.6M). The 8-Ks show continuing
  management turnover (Aug 10, Sep 10, Sep 16).

**What the price assumes.** At $35.84 the stock is at its 52-week low, 53% below the high, with 9% of the float short. At
16.6x forward earnings against a footwear peer median of 11.2x (Deckers 9.3x, On 14.4x, Lululemon 11.2x, Adidas 12.5x), it
still carries a brand premium. A turnaround is expected; the numbers don't yet show one.

**Thursday's trade in numbers.** The options price an 8.6% move (Oct 2 straddle $3.10 on $35.84; implied vol 104%). The last
15 reports averaged 8.7% (median 6.8%): fairly priced. Direction has skewed down: 6 of the last 9 reactions were negative, the worst
-20% and -15.5%. The bull case is positioning: a heavily shorted stock at its low can squeeze on "less bad" (+15% in June 2025).

**What decides it:** North America wholesale and sell-through, the size of the China decline, the Q2 revenue and margin guide,
any further tariff-refund effects in the numbers, and inventory.
