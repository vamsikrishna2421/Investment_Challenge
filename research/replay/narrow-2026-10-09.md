## Reading

- Picked after the fact (hindsight: the whole record, future included), the names where the rules worked lift the paper rules from +84% to +118% and the real-book rules from +54% to +329% (+4.7% a month compounded): what a backtest that chooses its markets after seeing the results shows.
- Picked as it could have been done live (walk-forward: only trades closed before each session), 9 of the 10 versions made less than the unfiltered rules over 700 sessions. The exception (paper rules, names with a positive average over the prior 252 sessions: +121%) had a lower mean over the 33 separate hourly windows (+2.9% against +3.1%; +2.0% against +3.0% in the 21 windows with a year of record before them), so it fails RUNBOOK 2d's rule.
- At the signal level past success did not pick the next winners: in 13 of the 15 comparisons the allowed names' next trades did worse than the excluded names' (the two exceptions, +0.34 and +1.6 points, inside the noise). Names' first-half and second-half averages had rank correlations of 0.17 (paper radar), -0.04 (real radar) and 0.03 (night sleeve). A higher past win rate did not carry over: allowed names then won 21.3% against 20.2% (paper), 18.4% against 19.5% (real radar), 56.9% against 62.8% (night).
- The live rules' monthly rate: paper +2.83% a month on average (compounded +2.03%; monthly standard deviation 13%, so the 95% range of the true average is -1.6% to +7.3%), +0.04% a month over the last 12 months; real-book rules +1.91% (+1.58%; 8.7%), +0.95% over the last 12; the S&P 500 +1.50% (3.5%), the Nasdaq-100 +1.92% (4.9%). Upper bounds: the candidate list is today's, chosen with hindsight.
- Not adopted: the live rules stay.
