H-1B & cash-account guardrails

An H-1B holder may invest in US securities, and no law caps how often you trade your own account, including pre-market and after-hours sessions. What the visa bars is unauthorized employment, so the lines not to cross are trading for other people, being paid for it, or running it as a business (for example electing trader tax status). Gains are short-term capital gains, taxed as ordinary income for a US tax resident; as an NRI for Indian tax purposes, US trading gains aren't taxed in India.
